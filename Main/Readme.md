📊 Current Project

For the first few learning days, I am working with a Retail Sales dataset representing transactions from a retail business.

The dataset contains information such as:

- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer information
- Product category
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Total sales
- Payment method

The goal is to understand how a real-world analyst would inspect, clean and explore business data before producing insights.

🏢 Real-World Business Context

Imagine a retail company wants to understand:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform well?
- How many units are being sold?
- Are there missing or duplicate transactions?
- Which payment methods are commonly used?
- What patterns can be found in customer purchases?

These are the types of questions that can lead from raw data to useful business decisions.
